About

Hi, I'm Andrej.

Over a decade shipping across VR, console, PC and mobile, at Sumo Digital, Maze Theory, 22cans, PRELOADED and UNIT9. I came into production through IT infrastructure, including projects for Google, and a computer science degree, so I plan around what the build actually takes. My background in anthropology helps teams understand the why behind their work, not just the what.

Through my studio LudoSpark I run production training for studios and games R&D for clients including Greenpeace. I'm based in Bucharest and work in English, Romanian and Italian.

Most production work happens in the gap between what the plan says and what the build actually is. That gap is where my effort goes.

  1. DiscoverKick-off call, outcomes agreed, access shared.Kick-off call.   2. AssessI play, read and interview your leads.I play the build, read every document and interview your leads, then map the plan against the build, the budget and the runway.
  3. RecommendA written report and a walkthrough call.You get a written report and a 90-minute walkthrough call. Each finding has an owner, a recommendation and the decision you need to make. You choose what to act on, and creative direction stays with your team.
  4. Support · optionalOngoing help, with a handover at the end.I stay on as Producer on call or Embedded producer, with a steady rhythm of sessions and a monthly review. Everything lives in your tools, so your team owns it when we finish.

The first three steps are the two-week project assessment. You keep the report whatever you decide next.

In the first call we write down three outcomes for your project, in your terms. For example: “Milestone 3 accepted by the publisher on 14 March”, “Scope fits the remaining runway”, “Port passes certification on first submission”. Every report and review checks progress against them.

Against the three outcomes we agree at the start. Each monthly review shows where each one stands, in plain numbers and dates.

Yes, before I see anything. I can also send my own if you’d rather.

Do you replace our producer?

Producer on call works alongside your producer as senior backup. Embedded producer fills the role when you don’t have one. Either way, your team keeps ownership of the plan.

Do you work with publishers?

Yes. Publishers use the project assessment to get an outside view of a developer’s build, plan and team before they sign, and I can act as the producer on a funded external team.

Can you talk to our publisher or licensor?

Yes. I can join milestone reviews and approval meetings, and prepare the reporting they expect.

How does working across time zones work?

Calls are booked in slots that suit Europe, the US and Asia-Pacific, and written updates keep everyone informed between calls.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. You get a full refund up to the end of the kick-off call.

How does payment work?

Half on signing and half when the report is ready. The walkthrough call and the written report follow the second payment. The fee comes off your first invoice if you keep me on.
